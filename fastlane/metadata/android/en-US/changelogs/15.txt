Design polish.

The "Done" button on the app-selection screen moved to the top bar, so the keyboard no longer covers it while you search, and the list keeps its last rows reachable above the keyboard.

The breathing animation on the pause screen is more organic: the breath ripples outward through the field with a quicker inhale and a longer exhale, the rotation sways gently instead of turning rigidly, and the whole aura drifts softly around its centre.
