A few small fixes.

When choosing apps, there's now a clear "Done" button to move forward — no need to reach for Back.

The intro now talks about the moment in between, rather than "a pause".

On the pause screen, everything stays in its place, but the four buttons — your three intentions and the "leave it" button — appear in a different order each time, so you actually read them instead of tapping from habit.

The breathing animation is now a soft field of little dots that drift and glow — something gentle to rest your eyes on.
