A calmer pause and a clearer killer feature.

The pause screen now asks one thing — your intention — with a slow breathing animation to settle into the moment. The per-pause time-limit question is gone.

Every app tile on the Home screen now shows a visible "pin to home screen" button, so it's obvious you can pin an app and have every tap route through the pause.

Time limits and their reminder notification were removed, and with them the notification permission. The app now requests no runtime permissions at all.
