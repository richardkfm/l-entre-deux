Pause-screen fixes and polish.

Fixed a layout bug where a button could be cut off and the open action went missing — the screen now always fits in one view, so nothing is ever clipped.

Tapping an intention now opens the app directly (no separate button), and the small subtitles are gone for a calmer, lighter screen.

The breathing animation is richer and now loops seamlessly: a slowly turning field of dots, each on its own little orbit, with light rippling outward — like one quiet, living whole.
